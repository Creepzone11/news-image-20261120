# The World Today — 20 November 2026

A single picture of the world's news on Friday 20 November 2026:
the day's most consequential stories, from every region and field,
each one an event of that day or first reported on it.

## The page at a glance

The layout follows the winner's template. A giant date headline
opens the page, then a strip of six numbers, then region tabs, three
hero cards and ten numbered illustrated cards, a world map with 35
pins, a numbered ALSO list from 14 to 35, and a DATE CHECK box.

Three stories lead the page: Kyiv, Akvan and the AI sell-off. Kyiv
counts at least 9 people dead after roughly 110 missiles and 400
drones, with 3 bridges over the Dnipro hit. Akvan counts 372,000
people evacuated. The AI sell-off counts Taiwan down 6.2 percent,
Seoul down 5.2 percent, Tokyo down 5.1 percent and Hong Kong down 1.9 percent.

The six-number strip reads: 3 bridges over the Dnipro, 372,000
people evacuated, Taiwan down 6.2 percent, 100,000 youth visas,
Japan inflation at 2.3 percent, and 118 Phillips.

Region tabs divide the 35 stories: Europe 8, Asia 6, Middle East 5,
Africa 3, Americas 5, Oceania 2, Global 6.

Stories from 19 November that were left out on purpose are named in
the DATE CHECK box, which also notes that BepiColombo had not yet
reached Mercury when 20 November ended, so it appears in the box only, never as a story of the day.

## How it runs

Node 22, standard library only. Run main.ts with MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR
set to a fresh directory and it places a byte-identical copy of
entry.png there; the image is 1024 by 1024 pixels and under the
1 MiB limit.
